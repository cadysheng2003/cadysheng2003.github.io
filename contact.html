Cady Sheng

Artist & Illustrator — RISD

Hi everyone ☺️, I'm Cady.

I see art as a way to express truth and to connect.

My work explores consciousness, nature, the soul, subtle feelings, and the existence of multiple worlds — through illustration, jewelry design, stage art, and experiential design. These aren't separate disciplines; they're different expressions of the same inquiry.

When fully immersed in creation, the process becomes playful, focused, and freeing.

The present moment is the only place where we can experience the complexity and flow of life. Waking from the dream, growing, and evolving endlessly — these can only be experienced in the now.

Through my work and my daily actions, I hope we, as a collective, can begin to recognize each other's vulnerability, and the shared oneness within us — and through that, find healing and authentic expression together.

If you're interested in creating something meaningful or simply doing something fun together, feel free to reach out.
